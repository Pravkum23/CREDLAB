## Why Intel is an informative case

Intel demonstrates why CREDLAB does not collapse credit analysis into a single score. The normalized filing shows a clear cash-generation pressure point, but the comparable EBITDA input is incomplete. A responsible system should state what it knows, calculate only what the inputs support, and leave the unsupported leverage conclusion unavailable.

## Analyst interpretation

The observed evidence identifies cash generation as the immediate credit question. Negative FCF reduces internally generated capacity for debt reduction, while the cash balance provides a partial buffer. The missing comparable D&A input means the MVP cannot responsibly state an EBITDA-based leverage conclusion for the period.

**Key question:** Can free cash flow recover enough to support debt reduction and refinancing needs?
