# CREDLAB project rules

1. Never invent financial data.
2. Every analytical conclusion must be traceable to source data.
3. Never imply CREDLAB produces an official credit rating.
4. Separate observed facts from calculated metrics.
5. Separate calculated metrics from analyst interpretation.
6. Preserve historical periods accurately.
7. Never introduce look-ahead information into historical analysis.
8. Sector-specific methodology may override generic methodology later.
9. All material calculations require tests.
10. Prefer explainability over opaque scores.
11. AI may explain evidence but may not fabricate evidence.
12. Missing data must be visible, not silently estimated.
13. Any assumption must be clearly labeled.
14. Research heuristics must be documented.
